Drive stats and the leaderboard now record every drive, including on a comma that turns off with the car. Includes everything in v0.0.58-alpha.4.

- Each drive is measured while you drive and saved as it goes: every 30 seconds and whenever the car comes to a stop. Earlier runtimes only recorded a drive 30 seconds after it ended, so a comma that powers off with the car lost almost every drive, and the leaderboard had nothing to show.
- The leaderboard sees a drive while it happens: while driving, the comma signs new totals about every 5 minutes, so This week fills in during the drive rather than at the next one.
- comma.ai's lifetime figure (Lifetime · comma.ai) is refreshed while driving too, not only while parked.
- Drives recorded by earlier runtimes are kept. Driving insights shows the drive in progress as before and adds it when it ends.

Testing notes: install this runtime from CommaView Android 0.0.162-alpha.5 (no app update is needed), make sure the comma is on the leaderboard, and drive. Within a few minutes of driving, This week should show your distance. Switch the car off and on again: the drive should still be there with its full distance. A drive should carry on, not start over, if the runtime restarts mid-drive.

This is an alpha for device validation. The public beta remains unchanged.
