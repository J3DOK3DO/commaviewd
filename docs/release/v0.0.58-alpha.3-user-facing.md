Live view no longer gets stuck reconnecting after a phone or head unit drops off the network, paired with CommaView Android 0.0.162-alpha.4.

- A camera streams to one device at a time. The same device reconnecting now replaces its own earlier connection instead of being turned away, so Live View no longer loops on "Reconnecting… Attempt 1" after a Wi-Fi change or a restart.
- A connection that vanished without closing (Wi-Fi lost, head unit powered off, switching between a phone hotspot and home Wi-Fi) frees its camera within about ten seconds.
- When another device has the camera open, the support report now says which one (`client rejected` in the bridge log).

Testing notes: on a comma 3X and a comma four, open Live View, then switch the head unit or phone between a hotspot and home Wi-Fi, or power it off and on: video should come back within about ten seconds. Open Live View on a second device at the same time: the second one should wait while the first streams.

This is an alpha for device validation. The public beta remains unchanged.
